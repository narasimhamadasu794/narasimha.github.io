THE PROBLEM

When everyone checks results at once.

Result announcements can create sudden traffic spikes that traditional systems may struggle to handle.

Traffic Surge

Thousands of students may request their results within a very short period of time.

Backend Overload

Sending every request directly to the same backend can increase processing pressure dramatically.

Long Waiting

Users may experience slow responses or repeated refreshes when the system becomes overloaded.

Uncontrolled Demand

Without traffic coordination, a temporary spike can affect the entire service.

THE SOLUTION

Control traffic before traffic controls the system.

Smart Result Traffic Manager introduces an intelligent coordination layer between users and result backends.

01 / ROUTING

Intelligent Routing

Incoming requests are distributed across healthy backend servers instead of concentrating demand on one destination.

02 / QUEUE

Smart Queue

When demand exceeds current processing capacity, requests are safely queued with a visible position.

03 / SCALING

Automatic Scaling

Additional backend capacity can be activated when queue pressure or utilization rises.

04 / HEALTH

Backend Health

Unhealthy backends can be detected and removed from active traffic distribution.

05 / AI

AI Traffic Control

AI-assisted release timing and traffic prediction help prepare infrastructure for expected demand.

06 / HISTORY

Request Analytics

Requests can be recorded and analyzed to understand traffic behavior and system performance.
